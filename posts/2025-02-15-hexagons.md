So you may have seen a new addition to the layout type when filling in the generator. That’s right, Hexagons will be soon making their way to the generator!

![Image showing hexagons as an upcoming available option](images/hexagons.png)

There will be a few requirements for hexagons to work though:

    Images MUST be cropped to just the texture, same as with the other layouts
    The background must be transparent and saved as a .PNG
    Textures must be oriented with the flat edge at the top and bottom

A good tool for cropping and removing the background, is removebg. I’m not affiliated, but have used them and know them to be a powerful and useful tool.

Once I’ve programmed it in, and tested it to check it works, I’ll release it to be used by everyone.

In the meantime, any other feedback you may have, please let me know when the feedback form.
